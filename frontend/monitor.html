How it works

Smooth water reflects the radar away and looks dark. On every pass we map the water at 20 m and look for a continuous water path from each community to the hub town, measuring its narrowest point. Radar does not measure depth: a route can exist and still be too shallow for boats, so narrow routes are flagged as at risk. Places that never show a resolvable water link are left out of the counts.
